Decision Log — Where a Direction Got Chosen
Structural
Eight phases collapsed to six
Professional Community was folded into the AI Fluency Push — the Push's own lede already treated community as its setup, so they were arguing one point in two boxes. Both open-slot / blank phases were then deleted rather than backfilled. All of Community's material was preserved into the merged phase first, not dropped, then trimmed deliberately over later turns.
Content
The CFB dynasty relocated out of Phase 01 — takeaways and all
Phase 01 was rewritten as a genuine skeptic's entry (2023 test sessions → Sora 2 → deciding to learn), which no longer had room for the college-football-dynasty origin. Rather than delete it, the dynasty and its "play / low-stakes / output tracks input" takeaways were moved into Initial Applications, where that evidence actually belongs. Content followed the argument, not the outline.
Structural / Content
The component callout format, retired in two moves
Both special "moment" callouts were dissolved back into normal content: "Where the Thesis Comes From" became a Phase 03 Takeaway; the "Front-End Breakthrough" became Phase 04's first What-Happened beat. The bordered-callout pattern (and later its warm color) stopped earning its distinctness once the surrounding copy carried the same weight. The CSS was left in place, unused.
Content
The thesis moved to the end and fully rewritten
"Your outputs are a reflection of your thinking" started mid-arc in Phase 05, tagged "The Thesis of the Whole Thing." It was relocated to the top of Phase 06 and re-tagged "Where I've Landed," so the arc builds toward the idea instead of announcing it early. The body was swapped out entirely — from the Jarvis-Ceiling shorthand to a self-contained argument that needs no prior framing.
Content / Tone
Lessons and summary ledes regenerated wholesale, once
After the reorder and the copy build, the oldest blocks on the page — the Lessons tiles and the phase ledes — still argued the pre-reorder story (Phase 02 still talked about credentials-as-translation-devices; Phase 04 still said "before any credential"). All of Phases 01–05 were regenerated in one pass to 2–3 distilled, evidence-backed principles each, matched to what the phases had actually become.
Structural / Visual
The desktop layout was fixed by screenshot, not by assumption
The Relevant Builds boxes were stretching to the O&E's height and filling with blank space. The fix — content-sized builds paired beside the Lessons tile, full-width O&E, and removing a global height:100% stretch — was checked by rendering the actual pages in a headless browser and measuring box heights, then re-checking mobile, before it shipped.
Structural
Phase 06 built as its own template
Flagged early as "fundamentally different," Phase 06 was given a separate, section-based render path rather than the 01–05 bento — an app suite, certifications, and a "keeping the flame lit" block, with the thesis banner up top. The app suite grew into eight clickable tiles that open a modal color-themed to each logo, verified end-to-end (click → themed panel → Esc-to-close) before shipping.

Cuts & Reversals — Built, Then Removed
  • "Courses & Resources" tile, present on every phase from the first build — removed once it stopped earning its space. The underlying data was left in the file, unrendered.
  • Mobile 2-column bento — built and shipped, then reverted on the very next turn. The single-column scroll simply felt smoother; structural mirroring lost to feel.
  • "New Workflows" section (Phase 06) — added as a placeholder box, then cut before it ever got content. Trimmed rather than filled; Other Certifications widened to take the row.
  • "Questions Moving Forward" box (Phase 06) — removed. The five questions were left dormant in the data, not deleted.
  • Two open-slot / blank phases — deleted outright in the 8→6 collapse. Held open early, then judged not worth holding.
  • The .component callout format — both instances dissolved into normal blocks. Its styling now sits unused; kept for a possible future callout.
  • A 192px "goblin-hub" placeholder tile — shipped for exactly one turn, then replaced by the full-resolution Link Hoarder image. Named and swapped once the real title and a higher-res file arrived.
  • "Salesforce teams ping" / "Utilization of…" — unfinished notes handed over mid-list, never published. Left out on purpose rather than shipping a fragment; later dropped entirely.

The unglamorous truth of the session

The page's whole argument is that outputs are a reflection of the thinking behind them. The commit log is the uncensored version of that claim: sixty-four pushes, three reversals, a mobile layout built and then unbuilt, a miscommunication settled only by a screenshot, and a thesis that had to be carried to the very end before it read right. Most of those sixty-four were not new ideas — they were someone changing their mind on purpose, one small correction at a time. That is what the finished page is actually made of.
