The Thesis — the Two Full Rewrites
The lines carrying the most weight were replaced outright, not tweaked.
Masthead sub-line — from description to argument
Not a one-off course. An exponential arc — each phase built from the last. Click a phase to open it.
I thought courses would be what moved me forward. What actually made these insights last was the combination — knowledge, real experience, and the right human conditions — compounding across every phase.
Thesis body — from named-framework shorthand to self-contained
The ceiling rises with the evaluative capacity of the person directing it — the Jarvis Ceiling. This is why fluency cannot be delivered by a tool rollout: the tool is constant, the thinking is the variable… Threat literacy over tech fluency. Human first.
AI doesn't create quality where none existed — it amplifies and executes whatever thinking you bring to it… The tool has no ceiling of its own — the ceiling is the thinker, not the tool… What comes out is just what you put in, reflected back at a different scale.
The idea is unchanged; the version that assumed you already knew "the Jarvis Ceiling" was swapped for one that explains itself to a first-time reader.
Cuts & Reversals — Built, Then Removed
  • "Courses & Resources" tile, on every phase from the start — removed once it stopped earning its space. Data left in the file, unrendered.
  • Mobile 2-column bento — built and shipped, reverted the next version. The single-column scroll simply felt smoother.
  • "New Workflows" section (Phase 06) — added as a placeholder, cut before it got content. Other Certifications widened to take the row.
  • "Questions Moving Forward" box (Phase 06) — removed. The five questions left dormant in the data.
  • Two open-slot / blank phases — deleted in the 8→6 collapse. Held open early, then judged not worth holding.
  • The bordered "component" callout format — both instances dissolved into normal blocks. Its styling now sits unused.
  • A 192px "goblin-hub" placeholder tile — live for exactly one version, replaced by the full-res Link Hoarder image. Swapped once the real name and file arrived.
  • "Salesforce teams ping" / "Utilization of…" — unfinished notes, never published. Left out on purpose rather than shipping a fragment.
What the redlines actually show

Very little of this landed on the first pass. The typos got cleaned, the app names got reconciled, one label was shipped wrong and pulled back, a sentence had "relationship" moved forward by hand, and the two most important lines on the page — the sub-head and the thesis — were thrown out and rewritten from scratch. Sixty-four versions, and most of the distance between the first and the last is exactly this: small, deliberate corrections, made on purpose.
