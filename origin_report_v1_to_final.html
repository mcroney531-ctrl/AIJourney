The Four Milestones
The 36 files collapse to three unique pre-git states; the fourth is where the repo ended.
v1"AI Fluency & Organizational Readiness"Honcho one-pager
A static, single-column bio timeline — cream paper, serif type (Playfair / DM Sans), a red accent, and a "More Sections To Come" placeholder. Built for the Honcho meeting specifically, not a general audience. No phases, no interactivity, no teal. A different artifact wearing the same ambition.
v2"The Progression"warm palette · first hub
The first interactive version: a real phase model, eight phases, a "Work Tie-Ins" block, and the thesis already present in Jarvis-Ceiling form. Still on the old warm/cream palette. The concept flipped here — from a one-pager to a navigable hub.
v14"The Progression"teal tracker · repo start
The unified teal system, the segmented pill tracker, and a deliberately empty "Open Slot" phase. This is the exact file that opened Session 2 and became commit #1. Everything after this point is captured in git.
Final"The AI Arc"shipped
Six phases, the thesis moved to the end and rewritten, an eight-tile app suite with color-themed modals, and a Phase 06 built on its own template. 5,415 words of content, from a 270-word start.

Zero Words, Same DNA
v1 shares 0% of its phrasing with the final — and yet the final is unmistakably built from it. What carried was the thinking, not the text.
  • v1: "The certification was one stop. This is the larger arc it belongs to."  became the entire framing — and the name "The AI Arc."
  • v1: "Delaware Dynasty — CFB Simulation"  became the Phase 03 entry story and its personal-first takeaway.
  • v1: UMD certification + "recognized publicly by Honcho"  became Phase 02 and the reason the whole document exists.
  • v1: QuickSum, LiftBuddy, TaskCalendar, "First & Learn"  became the app suite — three survived, "First & Learn" was cut, and GitGet, ProjDash, FoodLog, Command GM & Link Hoarder were added.
  • v1: a Honcho-specific one-pager  became a general-audience case study — the memoir→evidence reframe started as an audience pivot.

The whole distance, in one line

The finished page and its first draft share their subject, their evidence, and one sentence's worth of idea — and not a single phrase of actual text. Everything between them, from a 270-word cream one-pager to a 5,415-word teal app, is the thinking being reworked over and over until the words caught up to it. That is the argument the page makes, told by its own edit history.
