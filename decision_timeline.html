Why it takes three sources

No single record holds the whole build. The snapshots caught three still frames. The transcript caught the reasoning between them — including two prototypes that were built and thrown away without ever being saved. Git caught the last 64 moves exactly. Lined up on one spine, they show the truth the finished page only implies: almost none of it was decided once.
